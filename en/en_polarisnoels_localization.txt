/* ___ Desc_multiplayer_enemy ___ */
Enemy summon mode is the synchronization methods in multiplayer battle
Starter only: Only the battle starter spawns enemies; other players synchronize with their enemies.
Smart Average: Dynamically adjusts the total number of monsters using an algorithm to create a more relaxed monster-fighting environment.
Independent Spawn: Completely independent spawning, go ahead and fight freely!  *May cause lag when too many players
*Cannot be changed after the battle starts
*Danger incrases the same as amount of enemies
